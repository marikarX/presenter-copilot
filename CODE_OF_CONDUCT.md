# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in Presenter Copilot to be professional, constructive, and welcoming.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- discussing technical and product disagreements on their merits;
- giving specific, actionable feedback;
- respecting different levels of experience and communication styles;
- avoiding harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or personal attacks;
- protecting confidential or sensitive information shared in good faith;
- accepting maintainer decisions even when you disagree with them.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment or discriminatory conduct;
- sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention;
- intimidation, threats, stalking, or publication of private information;
- deliberate disruption of discussions or development activity;
- bad-faith security disclosures;
- repeated personal attacks or abusive language.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues, or other contributions that violate this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

If a conduct issue cannot safely be reported publicly, use a private repository contact or GitHub's private reporting mechanisms where available.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, project chat spaces, and other spaces where someone is representing the project.
