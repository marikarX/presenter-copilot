`create_authored_slide` operation with normal scope/evidence/privacy validation.

current target preconditions. The workspace heading/picker use the loaded revision's slide count.

per-slide Points, Two columns, Cards, or Statement layouts. Statement accepts
at most one point; all layouts retain the title, every point, and speaker notes.
Templates use ordinary editable text boxes, title placeholders, and rectangle
panels, explicit Arial fonts and RGB colors, with no external assets. Missing

provider outlines. Core rejects unknown choices before importing a document.

editing; no new provider or credential pathway is introduced. Native theme-layout discovery, applying used/unused layouts, placeholder remapping
and insertion are implemented; applying the app creation templates wholesale to
imported decks remains open.

creation with a slide-specific message. Expanding a Statement beyond one point

content/order/notes; per-slide layout choices must be selected again afterward.

## Current implementation status — 2026-10-02

The desktop app contains a limited research preview and early PW1 inventory.
Core validates the source snapshot, maps slide order and standard drawing
objects, reports counts by shape, picture, graphic frame, connector, and group,
and derives internal slide/object IDs from the imported document lineage plus
OOXML slide/shape IDs. These structural counts do not certify renderer fidelity.
The IDs remain stable when a managed
revision preserves its anchors; they do not link independently imported files.
Project schema v13 stores presentation lineage, immutable revisions, and the
current-revision head; binds supported rehearsal sessions to the revision they
started with; and scopes parsed slide units to a revision. Existing projects
migrate transactionally, with prior slide units assigned to their current head.
Source deletion cleans managed revision files.

After a text, speaker-notes, geometry, picture creation, crop or image-replacement edit, safe object or slide
creation/deletion/duplication, slide-order edit, or restore, the renderer asks Core to re-index the verified
